## Buttons

Use the homepage pill button classes by default for action buttons and CTA links. Apply `pill-button` with `pill-button--primary` for black primary actions or `pill-button--secondary` for secondary actions with background `#F0F0F3`. Keep the fully rounded shape at every breakpoint. The shared styles are in `src/app/_components/home-hero.css`.
